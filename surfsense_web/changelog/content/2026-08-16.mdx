## Reports and resumes have moved to artifacts

SurfSense now creates reports, resumes, and other deliverables through the artifacts system. Deliverables generated by the previous report and resume tools are no longer available after this upgrade. Ask SurfSense to regenerate an old deliverable to receive a new artifact that can be viewed and downloaded from the artifacts library.

PDF export has also been removed from the document editor. Documents can still be exported as DOCX, HTML, EPUB, ODT, LaTeX, or plain text; DOCX is now the default.
