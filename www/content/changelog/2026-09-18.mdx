## What's New in v2.0.0

v2.0.0 makes SurfSense a free, open-source desktop app that runs on your own computer. In July we said SurfSense would become a hosted platform for AI agents. We have changed course, and the hosted app is being retired. You ask questions across your documents and get cited answers, then turn the same documents into decks, reports, study material and podcasts. The index stays on your disk, you choose the model, and there is no account. Get it for Windows, macOS (Apple Silicon) or Linux from the [downloads page](/downloads).

### If you used the hosted app

- **Export before 18 October 2026**: The hosted app now only exports your data, and the sunset page walks you through it. Hosted accounts are deleted when the export window closes.
- **Import into the desktop app**: In Settings › General, find Import from SurfSense cloud, click Upload and pick the exported ZIP. Workspaces, folders and chats come across, and documents arrive as text that the app indexes on your computer. Original files and generated artifacts are not imported.
- **Old desktop app**: Versions 0.0.x don't update to v2.0 on their own. Install the new app from the [downloads page](/downloads).
- **Self-hosting**: The Docker stack stays open source and is now community-supported.

### Your documents, your model

- **Any model**: Use any OpenAI-compatible API, a local server such as LM Studio or Ollama, or let the app download a model for you. Settings › Models offers Qwen3 in six sizes, and Scan hardware lists more that fit your computer.
- **Prompts that fit the model**: Chat and Studio pick one of three prompt styles to suit your model, so small local models get short, strict instructions.

### Studio

- **Twelve formats**: Summary, Word, Slides, Spreadsheet, Web page, PDF, Mind map, Flashcards, Quiz, Podcast, Image and Infographic. Word, Slides and Spreadsheet are editable `.docx`, `.pptx` and `.xlsx` files.
- **Offline podcasts and images**: The bundled Kokoro voice reads podcasts on your computer. Download SD 1.5, SDXL Base or SDXL Turbo once to make images and infographics locally.
- **Built-in viewers**: PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets and slides open inside the app, and quiz and flashcard progress is saved. A running job can be cancelled.

### Private by default

- **Nothing leaves until you allow it**: Model downloads, remote providers and update checks stay off until you say yes. Settings › Network lists every destination with its own switch.
- **Keys in your keychain**: API keys are stored encrypted, with the secret held in your operating system's keychain.
- **No telemetry**: No usage tracking and no crash reporting.

### Licenses

- **The app is free**: The app and its updates cost nothing. A license adds paid plugins and priority support, and when it expires the app keeps working. No plugins ship in v2.0.0; the first one planned is the web data API from our July announcement.
- **A free 14-day license**: Enter your email on the [downloads page](/downloads) and the license file arrives by email, with no card needed. Add it in Settings › License, which checks it offline. The [license page](/license) resends a lost file, and plans are on the [pricing page](/pricing).

### No longer broken

These fixes shipped the same day as v2.0.1:

- The macOS app failed at startup because its build used a Python that couldn't load SQLite extensions.
- The app quit at startup when the keychain could no longer unlock its stored secret, for example after a reinstall. It now starts, and you enter your API keys again.
- The update check in v2.0.0 looked at the wrong release and found nothing. If you're still on v2.0.0, install the current version from the [downloads page](/downloads).
