After a year of building SurfSense, we've made the difficult decision to wind down the company. SurfSense Inc.'s last day of operations is 27 November 2026. Thank you to everyone who chose SurfSense and trusted us with their work.

SurfSense itself isn't going away. From version 2.4.0 the app is completely free, with no license needed, and it will stay free for the foreseeable future. Rohan Verma, who started SurfSense, will keep maintaining it as a personal side project for the time being.

## What changes

- **Every feature is free.** The jobs that needed a license, security questionnaires and contract redlines, are part of the free app. There are no licenses or trials any more.
- **All of it is open source.** The parts that weren't, the jobs included, are now Apache-2.0 like the rest of SurfSense on [GitHub](https://github.com/MODSetter/SurfSense). Updates continue, at a slower pace.
- **Online services end on 27 November.** That covers license activation and trials, the plugins SurfSense hosted on plugins.surfsense.com, and SurfSense's hosted web search. Third-party plugins keep working.
- **Later versions move to a new signature.** Releases after 2.4.0 will gradually be signed in Rohan's own name instead of SurfSense Inc.'s. Automatic updates keep working, though Windows may show the new publisher name and warn about fresh downloads for a while.

## What to do

- **Update to SurfSense 2.4.0.** In the app, open Settings › About and click Check now, or download it from the [downloads page](/downloads).
- **On a Mac with SurfSense 2.3.0 or earlier,** download 2.4.0 from the website once. Those versions can't install an update as large as 2.4.0.
- **Still on the old SurfSense desktop app (0.0.x)?** It doesn't move to the new app on its own, so download the new one from the [downloads page](/downloads).
- **To search the web,** add your own SearXNG server in Settings › Network. Links you paste still open without one.

## Your data

Your files, chats and index live on your own computer, so nothing changes for them. The license and trial records on SurfSense's servers will be deleted, as the [privacy policy](/privacy) describes.
