SurfSense is free and open source under the Apache License 2.0. The whole app, and every update, is free with no account and no expiry.

A SurfSense license adds:

- **SurfSense's paid plugins.** These read public sites for the agent, such as Google Search, YouTube and a web crawler. Adding sources from the web in [Sources](/docs/v2/sources) uses them too. The [plugins page](/plugins) lists them.
- **Jobs**, with a Business license or its trial. A job fills in paperwork from your firm's own documents, such as a security questionnaire from your policies. See [Jobs](/docs/v2/jobs).
- **Priority support.**

Nothing else needs a license. Chat, the agent, Studio, voice, local and remote models, third-party plugins and updates all work without one. See [pricing](/pricing) for plans.

## Free trial

You can try the paid plugins and [jobs](/docs/v2/jobs) free for 14 days, with no card, on a Business trial. Enter your email on the [downloads page](/downloads) and the trial license arrives by email as a file. One Business trial per email address, even if you tried the paid plugins before.

In the app, **Start a free trial** in Settings › License opens [pricing](/pricing) in your browser, and **Start 14-day trial** in Jobs opens the downloads page.

## Add your license

Your license is a file called `surfsense.lic`, sent to the email you bought it with. You can also download it from the page you land on after paying.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Save `surfsense.lic` from the email.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    In SurfSense, open Settings › License and choose **Add license**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Choose **Choose license file**, or paste the file's text under **Or paste the file**, then **Add**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Settings › License then shows your plan, your email, and **Expires** with the date. The sidebar shows **License active**.

If your license includes jobs, SurfSense then downloads them from its license server. The first time, it asks before it contacts `api.surfsense.com`: choose **Allow**. See [Get the job](/docs/v2/jobs#get-the-job).

A Business license shows as **Business plan**, or **Solo plan** when it is for one person. It also shows **Licensed to** with your firm's name, when one was given at checkout, how many people it covers, and **Jobs:** with the job plugins it unlocks, such as **Security questionnaire**, which you run from [Jobs](/docs/v2/jobs).

To swap in a new file, use **Replace license**. To take the license off this computer, use **Remove license**.

## Checked on your computer

SurfSense checks the license file on your computer, with no network. Checking it sends nothing anywhere.

When you use a paid plugin, your license key goes with each request to `plugins.surfsense.com`, so the plugin service can check it. Checking for a renewal, and getting the [jobs](/docs/v2/jobs) your license includes, send it to `api.surfsense.com`, SurfSense's license server ([below](#business-renewal)). **Check for renewal** sends it whatever your plan, whether you choose it in Settings › License or in Jobs. Those are the only two places your license key goes, and each host has to be allowed in Settings › Network first. See [privacy](/docs/v2/privacy).

## When it expires

When a license runs out, the paid plugins and its jobs stop. Everything else keeps working: the app, your data, what jobs made and your updates are never locked.

- A Business license ends 30 days after the year you paid for.
- In the last 14 days, Settings › License shows **Expiring soon**.
- After it ends, Settings › License shows **Expired** with **Renew license**, and the sidebar shows **License expired**. For a Business license, **Expired** points to **Check for renewal** instead, with **See pricing**.
- In [Jobs](/docs/v2/jobs), an ended Business license's jobs offer **Check for renewal**, and an ended trial's offer **See pricing** and **Replace license**.

For other plans, a renewal arrives by email as a new license file. Add it with **Replace license**.

## Business renewal

A Business license renews every year on the same key. Settings › License shows a **Renewal** row with **Check for renewal**, which sends your license key to `api.surfsense.com`, SurfSense's license server, replaces the file on this computer with the latest one, and downloads any job it adds. It then shows **Renewed until** with the new date, or **No renewal yet**.

The first time, SurfSense asks before it contacts `api.surfsense.com`. Allowing it also lets SurfSense check on its own when it starts and once a day: for a renewal, from 30 days before your Business license ends until 30 days after, so a renewal arrives before you need it, and for newer versions of your jobs, while your license includes them. A check for a job that comes during a run of it is made again once the run ends. To stop all of it, untick **License server** in Settings › Network.

Each renewal is also emailed to you as a license file. On a computer that stays offline, add it with **Replace license**.

## Lost your file?

Go to [surfsense.com/license](/license) and enter the email the license was issued to. SurfSense emails every license registered to that address. For your security the page says the same thing whether or not it found one.

If you can no longer reach that inbox, or mistyped it when buying, the same page says how to get help. A license bought for a team is sent only to the address that bought it, so ask that person to forward it.

## Messages you may see

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| This is not a SurfSense license file. | Check you chose `surfsense.lic`, or pasted all of it. |
| This license file was not issued by SurfSense, or it was altered. | Get a fresh copy from [surfsense.com/license](/license). |
| This license file has expired; request a fresh copy at surfsense.com/license. | Get a fresh copy from [surfsense.com/license](/license). |
| This license file uses an algorithm this version cannot check. | [Update SurfSense](/docs/v2/updates). |
| **Clock is off** | Your computer's clock is behind the last time SurfSense ran. Set the right time and the license works again. |
| **License not recognized** or **License revoked** on a plugin | The plugin service refused the key. Check that Settings › License shows the license you expect. A refunded license is revoked. |
| No license is added, so there is none to renew. | [Add your license](#add-your-license) first. |
| Allow SurfSense’s license server, api.surfsense.com, in Settings › Network, then try again. | Tick **License server** in Settings › Network, then choose **Check for renewal** again. |
| SurfSense’s license server has no license with this key. | Check that Settings › License shows the license you bought, or get a fresh copy from [surfsense.com/license](/license). |
| SurfSense’s license server will not renew this license. Contact support. | The license was suspended, as after a refund. Get help from [surfsense.com/license](/license). The app keeps working. |
| This license was checked too often; try again in an hour. | Wait an hour, then choose **Check for renewal** again. |
| SurfSense’s license server could not send a renewed file; try again later. | Try again later, or add the emailed file with **Replace license**. |
