SurfSense is free and open source under the Apache License 2.0. From version 2.4.0 every feature is free, with no license, account, trial or expiry: chat, the agent, Studio, voice, local and remote models, [adding sources from the web](/docs/v2/sources#add-from-the-web), plugins, updates and the [jobs](/docs/v2/jobs).

The code that used to be under the Business Source License, and the job packs, are Apache-2.0 from 2.4.0 too. The `LICENSE` files in the [repository](https://github.com/MODSetter/SurfSense) say which license covers which files.

The app also includes other people's open-source software, each under its own license. To read those licenses, go to Settings › About and choose **Third-party notices**.

## Why there are no licenses any more

Until 27 November 2026, SurfSense Inc. sold licenses for SurfSense's paid plugins and jobs. The company has wound down, and SurfSense carries on as a free, open-source project, maintained by its creator. The license server, trials and SurfSense's own paid plugins closed on that date. See [pricing](/pricing).

## If you had a license

You don't need it any more. [Update SurfSense](/docs/v2/updates) to 2.4.0 or later and everything works without it. There is nothing to add, renew or recover, and your sources, chats and settings stay where they are. Every renewal has been cancelled.

## On an older version

Versions before 2.4.0 still check for a license, so they may show messages such as **License expired**, **Expiring soon** or "This license file has expired; request a fresh copy at surfsense.com/license". Licenses can no longer be issued, resent or renewed. [Update SurfSense](/docs/v2/updates) instead, and the messages go away.

On a Mac with SurfSense 2.3.0 or earlier, download the new version from the [download page](/downloads) once: those versions can't install updates larger than 1 GiB by themselves.
