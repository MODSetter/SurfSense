Studio turns the sources you pick into something you keep: a Word document, a deck, a spreadsheet, a PDF, a web page and more. It sits in the right panel; if the panel is hidden, click **Show right panel** in the title bar. [Studio](/docs/v2/studio) lists every format.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Pick a format

Click the format you want, such as **Word**, **Slides**, **Spreadsheet** or **PDF**. A box opens with the sources it will use and a prompt.

![The Word box, with its sources button, an empty prompt and Generate](/guides/studio-documents/01-format-box.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose the sources

Click the sources button to see which sources it will use. They're the ones ticked in **Sources**, and the ticks here are the same ticks. Only sources that are ready can be used.

![The list of sources in the Word box, each one ticked](/guides/studio-documents/02-choose-sources.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Steer it and generate

Under **Prompt (optional)**, say what you want, such as "a one-page checklist", then click **Generate**. The new document appears under **Artifacts** while it's made, and you can keep working or start another.

![A Word document being made, at the top of the Artifacts list](/guides/studio-documents/03-generating.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Open and download it

When it's ready, click it to open it in the right panel. **Download** saves the file: a real `.docx`, `.pptx`, `.xlsx` or `.pdf` you can open and edit in Word, PowerPoint, Excel or LibreOffice.

![The Word document open in Studio's viewer, with Download at the top](/guides/studio-documents/04-word-viewer.webp)

</Step>
</Steps>

Studio uses the chat model in Settings at the moment you click **Generate**. A strong model lays out Word documents and PDFs more richly; a small local one makes simpler ones.
