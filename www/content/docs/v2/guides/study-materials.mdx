Studio makes study material from the sources you pick: flashcards to go through one at a time, a quiz with explanations, and a mind map of how the ideas connect. They stay in the workspace, and your progress is saved as you go.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Start all three

In Studio, click **Flashcards**, write a prompt if you want to steer it, and click **Generate**. Do the same for **Quiz** and **Mind map**: you can start several at once, and each appears under **Artifacts**.

![Flashcards, a quiz and a mind map being made, under Artifacts](/guides/study-materials/01-three-at-once.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Go through the flashcards

Open the flashcards. Each card shows a question; click **See answer** to turn it over.

![A flashcard showing its question, with See answer](/guides/study-materials/02-flashcard.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Mark what you know

Mark each card **Got it** or **Needs review**, and use **Shuffle** to change the order. The counts show how far you've got.

![A flashcard with its answer revealed, and the Needs review and Got it buttons](/guides/study-materials/03-flashcard-answer.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Take the quiz

Open the quiz and pick an answer. The quiz marks it right or wrong and shows the right answer; click **Next** to go on, or **Skip** a question. At the end you get your score, and **Retake quiz** runs it again, or only the questions you missed.

![A quiz question answered: the wrong pick marked, the right answer shown, and Next](/guides/study-materials/04-quiz.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Explore the mind map

Open the mind map to see your sources' main ideas as branches. Click a branch to fold or unfold it, and the button at the top fits the whole map in the panel.

![A mind map of the sources, branching from one idea in the middle](/guides/study-materials/05-mind-map.webp)

</Step>
</Steps>
