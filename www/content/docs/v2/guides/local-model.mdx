A model on your computer keeps your questions and documents on it, and works offline once it's downloaded. [On this computer](/docs/v2/models/local) has the details.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open Settings › Text gen

Click the gear at the bottom left to open **Settings**, then **Text gen** under **Models**. **In use:** names the model that answers in chat now.

![Settings, Text gen, with the model in use and the models on this computer](/guides/local-model/01-text-gen.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Pick a model that fits

Click **Add model** and scroll to **On this computer**. SurfSense shows what it found, such as **Graphics card** and its memory, and judges every model against it:

- A model with no badge runs at full speed.
- **Reduced speed** runs partly on the processor, so it's slower.
- **Won’t fit** needs more memory than this computer has.

The model with the ribbon is recommended for your computer.

![Models tested by SurfSense, each with its size and a Download button](/guides/local-model/02-fits-this-computer.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Download it

Click **Download** on the model you want. When it's done, it reads **In use**, and the next reply in any chat comes from it. To switch back later, click **Use** on another model.

![The downloaded model marked In use](/guides/local-model/03-downloaded.webp)

</Step>
</Steps>
