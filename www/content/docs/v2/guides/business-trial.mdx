---
title: Get the jobs
description: The jobs come with SurfSense from 2.4.0, free, with no trial or license to add
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

Jobs, such as [filling in a security questionnaire](/docs/v2/guides/security-questionnaire) or [redlining a contract](/docs/v2/guides/contract-redline), used to need a SurfSense Business license or its 14-day trial. From SurfSense 2.4.0 they are part of the free app: there is no trial to ask for and no license file to add. See [License](/docs/v2/license).

<Steps>
<Step>

### Update SurfSense

Open Settings › About and click **Check now**, or download the newest version from the [download page](/downloads). See [Updates](/docs/v2/updates).

</Step>
<Step>

### Open Jobs

Click **Jobs** in the left sidebar. The jobs are already there, installed by SurfSense when it starts, each with **Run**.

</Step>
</Steps>
