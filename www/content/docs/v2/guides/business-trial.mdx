title: Start a trial and add your license
description: Ask for the free 14-day Business trial, add the license file it sends, and let SurfSense download your jobs
---

<GuideVideo slug="business-trial" poster="/guides/business-trial/poster.webp" title="Adding a trial license in Settings › License, then allowing SurfSense to download its jobs" />

Jobs, such as [filling in a security questionnaire](/docs/v2/guides/security-questionnaire), come with a SurfSense Business license, and with its free 14-day trial. Everything else in SurfSense needs no license. See [License](/docs/v2/license) for the plans.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Ask for the trial

Enter your email on the [downloads page](/downloads), and the trial's license file arrives by email. No card is needed, and each email address gets one trial. In the app, Settings › License says "No license on this device", and **Start a free trial** there opens [pricing](/pricing) in your browser.

![Settings › License with no license](/guides/business-trial/01-no-license.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Add the license file

Save `surfsense.lic` from the email. In Settings › License, click **Add license**, then **Choose license file** and pick it. Or paste the file's text under **Or paste the file** and click **Add**.

![The Add license dialog](/guides/business-trial/02-add-license.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Allow your jobs to download

SurfSense downloads the jobs your license includes from its license server, `api.surfsense.com`. The first time, it asks "Allow downloading your jobs?" and says what the server gets: your license key and your IP address, and each job's code and version. Click **Allow**.

![The question SurfSense asks before it downloads your jobs](/guides/business-trial/03-allow-jobs.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Check that it reads Active

Settings › License now says **Active**, with the date the trial ends and the jobs it includes. **Jobs on this computer** lists each job with its version once it's downloaded. A job that reads "Not downloaded yet" downloads with **Download**, here or on its row in **Jobs**.

![Settings › License with the trial active](/guides/business-trial/04-active.webp)

</Step>
</Steps>

When the trial ends, jobs stop running. To keep running them, buy a Business license on [pricing](/pricing), then replace the trial's license with the one we email you: click **Replace license** in Settings › License.
