The **Security questionnaire** job fills in a questionnaire a customer sent you, as an Excel workbook, from your firm's own policies. Each answer rests on a passage it quotes, and a question it can't back is left for a person. It comes with a Business license or its [free trial](/docs/v2/guides/business-trial). See [Jobs](/docs/v2/jobs) for everything a job does.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Add the workbook and your policies

Add the questionnaire, as an `.xlsx` file, and your policies to **Sources**, and wait until they're ready. Save a Word or older Excel file as `.xlsx` first. Here the questions are the NCSC's supplier questions, and the policy is a firm's written information security plan, already a source.

</Step>
<Step>

### Open Jobs

Click **Jobs** in the sidebar. A job that's ready shows **Run**.

![Jobs, with the security questionnaire ready to run](/guides/security-questionnaire/01-jobs.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose the workbook and the documents

Click **Run** beside **Security questionnaire**. Under **Workbook to fill in**, the workbook in your sources is picked: SurfSense fills in a copy and leaves your file as it is. Under **Your firm’s documents to answer from**, tick the documents the answers should rest on. Here that's **Deselect all**, then the security plan.

![The run form, with the workbook and the security plan chosen](/guides/security-questionnaire/02-run-form.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Start

At the foot of the form, check the model that answers: a model that doesn't run on your computer receives the questions and passages from your documents. Then click **Start**.

![The foot of the run form, naming the model, with Start](/guides/security-questionnaire/03-start.webp)

Jobs shows how far the run has got. You can close Jobs and keep working, but keep SurfSense open until the run is done. **Cancel run** stops it, and nothing is written.

![The run under way](/guides/security-questionnaire/04-progress.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Read what it did

When the run is done, Jobs says how many questions SurfSense answered, how many need a person, and which of its sheets to check before you send the copy.

![The finished run's summary](/guides/security-questionnaire/05-done.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Check the copy in Studio

Click **Open in Studio**. Each answer is in its cell, with a comment that names the passage it rests on. To read more of the sheet, hide the sidebar and drag Studio's edge to widen it.

![The filled-in questionnaire in Studio](/guides/security-questionnaire/06-answers.webp)

The **SurfSense exceptions** sheet lists every question a person should look at, and why. **SurfSense answers** has each answer's source and the quote it rests on.

![The exceptions sheet, listing the questions left for a person](/guides/security-questionnaire/07-exceptions.webp)

</Step>
</Steps>

SurfSense writes Yes only where a passage in your documents says so, word for word, and a second check agrees. Download the copy with **With changes**. Before you send it, check SurfSense's two sheets, and delete them if the recipient shouldn't see them.

Here the job answers with Claude Haiku 5.5 through OpenRouter. A model that runs on your computer keeps your documents on it, but it is usually smaller and leaves more questions for a person.
