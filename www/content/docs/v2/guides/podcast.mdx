---
title: Turn your sources into a podcast
description: Brief a podcast episode from your sources, let SurfSense voice it on your computer, and listen with its transcript
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<GuideVideo slug="podcast" poster="/guides/podcast/poster.webp" title="Briefing, making and playing a short podcast episode" />

Studio writes a conversation about your sources and voices it with a voice model on your computer. SurfSense ships one and picks it for you, so podcasts work offline from the start. See [Podcast voices](/docs/v2/voice#podcast-voices).

<Steps>
<Step>

### Brief the episode

In Studio, click **Podcast**. SurfSense prepares a brief from your sources: the language, a style such as **Conversational**, **Interview** or **Debate**, the length, and the speakers. Give each speaker a name, a role and a voice, or **Add speaker**.

![The podcast brief: language, style, length, two speakers with their voices, sources and a prompt](/guides/podcast/01-brief.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Generate it

Click **Generate**. Your chat model writes the script, then the voice model reads each line on your computer. A short episode takes a few minutes.

![The podcast being made, under Artifacts](/guides/podcast/02-generating.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Listen and read along

Open the episode to play it, with its transcript under the player. **Download** saves it as an MP3.

![The podcast open, with its player and its transcript by speaker](/guides/podcast/03-player.webp)

</Step>
</Steps>

A voice model on your computer checks for free memory before it starts, and says how much it needs if there isn't enough. Close other apps and try again, or pick a lighter voice model in **Settings › Audio**.
