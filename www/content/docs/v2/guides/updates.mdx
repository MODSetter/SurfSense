With update checks on, SurfSense checks for a newer version once a day by itself. You can check by hand at any time. See [Updates](/docs/v2/updates) for what a check sends and how each kind of install updates.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Check from the title bar

Click the small button beside the right panel toggle, at the top right. It shows **Check for updates** when you point at it, and says what it found, such as **SurfSense is up to date**.

![The title bar's update button and its result](/guides/updates/01-check.webp)

If you answered **No** to update checks when you set SurfSense up, it asks **Allow SurfSense to check for updates?** first. **Allow** also turns on the daily check.

</Step>
<Step>

### Or check in Settings › About

**Settings › About** shows the version you have. **Check now** checks again, and **Release notes** opens the notes for your version.

![Settings › About, with the version and Check now](/guides/updates/02-about.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Install it

When a newer version is out, SurfSense downloads it, and the title bar button turns into **Update**. Click it, or **Restart to update** in Settings › About, to install it now. Or carry on working: it installs the next time you quit. Your data folder is not touched.

</Step>
</Steps>

The title bar button doesn't appear when your organization or a package manager handles updates, or in Docker.
