<GuideVideo slug="agent-documents" poster="/guides/agent-documents/poster.webp" title="The agent writing a Word report from the sources, then changing it" />

In an **Agentic** chat, the agent works in steps: it searches your ticked sources, reads them in full when it needs to, and makes real Office files and PDFs. Each file appears in Studio. See [Agent](/docs/v2/agent).

<Steps>
<Step>

### Ask in an Agentic chat

Click **New chat** and choose **Agentic** under **Mode for this chat**. Ask for the file you want, such as "a two-page Word report for a small business owner". Each step shows above the reply as one line while the agent works.

![The agent's first steps above its reply: searching and reading the sources](/guides/agent-documents/01-working.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Read what it did

When it's done, the steps list everything it did, and the reply says what the document draws on. The step that made the file links to it.

![The finished reply, with the step that created the Word document](/guides/agent-documents/02-reply.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Open the document

Click the file in its step to open it in Studio. It's a real `.docx` you can download and edit in Word or LibreOffice.

![The agent's Word document open in Studio](/guides/agent-documents/03-document.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Ask for a change

Ask in the same chat, such as "add a short section on backups". The agent makes the document's next version, and the earlier one stays in Studio's version list.

![The document's next version, after the change was asked for in the chat](/guides/agent-documents/04-version-2.webp)

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  When the agent wants to check its pages as Office lays them out, or convert a document to PDF, it needs Office support. If it's off, the step says so and the chat offers **Turn on Office support**. See [Office support](/docs/v2/agent#office-support).
</Callout>

Agentic works best on strong models. Small local models can stall on multi-step work.
