The first time you open SurfSense, a short setup runs. You see it once, and you can change every choice in it later, except the embedding model. Haven't installed SurfSense yet? Start with [Install](/docs/v2/install).

<Steps>
<Step>

### Start setting up

On the welcome screen, click **Start setting up**.

![The welcome screen, with the Start setting up button under the heading](/guides/set-up/01-welcome.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Make your privacy choices

Answer **Yes** or **No** to **Check for updates** and to **Share usage statistics**, then click **Continue**. Nothing is sent until you choose, and **Show exactly what’s sent** lists what each one sends. See [Privacy](/docs/v2/privacy).

![Privacy choices, with Yes chosen for both questions and the Continue button](/guides/set-up/02-privacy-choices.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose a text generation model

This is the model that answers you in chat. Under **On this computer**, SurfSense lists the models that fit your computer and marks one **Recommended**. A smaller model downloads sooner; the recommended one answers better.

To use a model from a server or a plan you already pay for instead, use the options below the list. See [Use a model from a server or provider](/docs/v2/guides/connect-a-server).

![The list of models for this computer, with a Download button on each](/guides/set-up/03-text-model.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Allow the download

Click **Download** beside the model. The first time, SurfSense asks **Allow searching and downloading models?** and says what Hugging Face receives. Click **Allow**.

![The question Allow searching and downloading models?, with Cancel and Allow](/guides/set-up/04-allow-download.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Wait for the model

The model downloads, and once it's done it's the one in use: its row reads **In use**, and the bar at the bottom names it. Click **Continue**.

![The downloaded model marked In use, and the bar at the bottom naming it](/guides/set-up/05-model-ready.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose an embedding model

The embedding model finds what you ask about in your documents. The one that comes with SurfSense works well for English; pick a multilingual one if your documents are in other languages. You can't change it later. Click **Finish**.

![The embedding models, with the one that comes with SurfSense in use](/guides/set-up/06-embedding-model.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Your first workspace opens

SurfSense opens a workspace called **My Workspace**: sources on the left, chat in the middle and Studio on the right.

![The first workspace, empty, with Sources, the chat box and Studio](/guides/set-up/07-first-workspace.webp)

</Step>
</Steps>
