The agent can work on your PDF sources and the PDFs it made: merge them, take pages out, split, rotate or reorder pages, add a watermark, page numbers, a header or a footer, and fill in forms. Each result is a new PDF in Studio. See [PDF tools](/docs/v2/agent#pdf-tools).

<Steps>
<Step>

### Ask in an Agentic chat

In an **Agentic** chat, say what to do, naming the PDFs, such as "merge these two into one PDF, then add page numbers". Each tool it uses is a step, such as "Merged PDFs" and "Numbered a PDF's pages".

![The agent's steps: merging the PDFs, then numbering the pages](/guides/pdf-tools/01-steps.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Open the result

The reply names the new PDF. Open it from **Artifacts** in Studio, and **Download** it from there. Your original PDFs stay as they were.

![The merged PDF open in Studio](/guides/pdf-tools/02-merged-pdf.webp)

</Step>
</Steps>

To fill in a form, tick it and ask: the agent reads the form's fields, fills them in, and can flatten the answers into the page. PDFs that need a password, signature fields and XFA-only forms are not supported. Converting a Word document or deck to PDF needs [Office support](/docs/v2/agent#office-support).
