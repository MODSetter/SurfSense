---
title: Add web pages and YouTube videos
description: Paste links or search the web, and add pages and videos as sources, read on your own computer
---

import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<GuideVideo slug="add-from-the-web" poster="/guides/add-from-the-web/poster.webp" title="Adding a web page and a YouTube video as sources" />

A web page becomes its text, and a YouTube video becomes its transcript, in the language the video speaks. SurfSense reads them on your own computer and connection, as your browser would. No license is needed. [Add from the web](/docs/v2/sources#add-from-the-web) has the details.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open Add from the web

Click **+** at the top of the **Sources** panel and choose **Add from the web…**.

![The Add from the web box, empty, with Web and YouTube under it](/guides/add-from-the-web/01-web-dialog.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Paste links, or search

Paste one or more links into the box and press Enter. To search instead, type words and press Enter; choose **YouTube** under the box to find videos. The first search asks to allow SurfSense's search server.

Each link or result gets an **Add** button.

![Two pasted links, a NIST page and a YouTube video, each with an Add button, and Add all (2)](/guides/add-from-the-web/02-links.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Allow the site

Click **Add**, or **Add all**. The first time you add from a site, SurfSense asks to allow it and says what the site receives: the page's address and your IP address, nothing from your documents. Click **Allow**. You can turn it off later in **Settings › Network**.

![The question Allow reading pages from www.nist.gov?, which also names www.youtube.com](/guides/add-from-the-web/03-allow-site.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Use them like any source

Close the box. Each page and video appears in **Sources** under its title and is ready once SurfSense has read it. They're searched and cited like your files.

![The web page and the video in Sources, ready and ticked, below the folder of guides](/guides/add-from-the-web/04-web-sources.webp)

</Step>
</Steps>

Some pages can't be read without a browser, such as pages behind a login. Save those as a PDF from your browser and [add the file](/docs/v2/guides/add-sources) instead.
