<GuideVideo slug="refine-and-versions" poster="/guides/refine-and-versions/poster.webp" title="Refining a Word document and switching between its versions" />

**Refine** rewrites a Word document, PDF or web page that Studio made into its next version, and the earlier one stays as it was. Documents the agent made are changed in its chat instead. See [Versions](/docs/v2/studio#versions).

<Steps>
<Step>

### Describe the change

Open the document, click the round **Refine** button at the bottom right, and describe a change, such as "shorter: five items". Press Enter.

![The Refine box over a Word document, with a change typed in](/guides/refine-and-versions/01-refine-box.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Read the new version

Studio rewrites the document with your chat model, and the viewer opens the new version. The version button at the top shows its number, such as **v2**.

![The refined Word document, shown as version 2](/guides/refine-and-versions/02-version-2.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Open an earlier version

Click the version button to list every version, and pick one to open it. Deleting the document deletes all of its versions.

![The version list open, with v1 and v2](/guides/refine-and-versions/03-versions.webp)

</Step>
</Steps>

A very long document may be too long for a small model to rewrite in one go: Studio says so and names the model.
