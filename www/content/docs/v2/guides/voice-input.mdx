import { Step, Steps } from 'fumadocs-ui/components/steps';

<GuideVideo slug="voice-input" poster="/guides/voice-input/poster.webp" title="Dictating a question into the chat box" />

Voice input uses the speech recognition model from [Turn a recording into a source](/docs/v2/guides/transcribe-a-recording). Your voice never leaves your computer; the text you send is an ordinary message. See [Voice input](/docs/v2/voice#voice-input-in-the-composer).

<Steps>
<Step>

### Speak

Click the microphone beside **Send** and speak. A recording strip appears in the chat box, and your words arrive a phrase at a time, after each pause.

![The chat box while dictating, with the words so far](/guides/voice-input/01-dictating.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Keep the text

Click **✓** or press Enter to finish: SurfSense adds the last phrase and leaves the text in the box. Nothing is sent until you send it. Press Escape or click **✕** to throw the recording away.

![The dictated question in the chat box, ready to send](/guides/voice-input/02-inserted.webp)

</Step>
</Steps>

The first time, your system may ask to let SurfSense use the microphone. Voice input stops after 5 minutes.
