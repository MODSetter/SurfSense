The **Contract redline** job reviews a contract you received, as a Word file, against your firm's playbook, one review point at a time, and marks up a copy with tracked changes and comments. Where your playbook says nothing on a point, the contract is left as it is there. It comes with a Business license or its [free trial](/docs/v2/guides/business-trial). See [Review a contract](/docs/v2/jobs#review-a-contract) for everything it does.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Add the contract and your playbook

Add the contract, as a `.docx` file, and your playbook to **Sources**, and wait until they're ready. Your playbook is your firm's written positions, its own standard agreement, or both. Here the contract is a real non-disclosure agreement from an SEC filing, and the playbook is the Bonterms Mutual NDA, a published standard agreement standing in for a firm's own.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose the contract and the playbook

In **Jobs**, click **Run** beside **Contract redline**. Under **Contract to review**, pick the contract: SurfSense marks up a copy and leaves your file as it is. Under **Your firm’s playbook**, tick your playbook's documents. Here that's **Deselect all**, then the Bonterms agreement.

![The run form, with the contract and the playbook chosen](/guides/contract-redline/01-run-form.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Start

At the foot of the form, check the model that reviews: a model that doesn't run on your computer receives the contract and passages from your playbook. Then click **Start**.

![The foot of the run form, naming the model, with Start](/guides/contract-redline/02-start.webp)

Jobs counts the review points as it checks them. You can close Jobs and keep working, but keep SurfSense open until the run is done.

![The run under way](/guides/contract-redline/03-progress.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Read what it did

When the run is done, Jobs says how many points it changed, how many already meet your playbook, how many your playbook doesn't cover, and how many it left for a person.

![The finished run's summary](/guides/contract-redline/04-done.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Check the copy in Studio

Click **Open in Studio**. Each change is a tracked change with a comment the other side can read. SurfSense's reasoning, with the words of your playbook it rests on, is an internal comment: it stays in Studio, and every download leaves it out. To read the comments beside the page, hide the sidebar, widen Studio, and scroll across.

![The marked-up contract in Studio, with its comments](/guides/contract-redline/05-redline.webp)

</Step>
</Steps>

Accept or reject the changes in Studio, with **Accept all** and **Reject all**, or in Word. Download the copy with **With changes**. Read every change before you send it: SurfSense changes only what your playbook takes a position on, so a playbook that says more catches more.

Here the job reviews with Claude Sonnet 5.5 through OpenRouter. A model that runs on your computer keeps the contract on it.
