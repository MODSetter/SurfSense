<GuideVideo slug="images" poster="/guides/images/poster.webp" title="Downloading an image model, drawing a picture and editing it" />

**Image** in Studio needs an image model. A model on your computer works offline and runs only while Studio needs it; with a graphics card a picture takes seconds, without one several minutes. See [Image models](/docs/v2/studio#image-models).

<Steps>
<Step>

### Choose an image model

Open **Settings**, then **Image gen**, and click **Add model**. Pick a model under **On this computer** and click **Download**, or use one from a server you connected.

![Image models to download on this computer, starting with FLUX.2 klein 4B](/guides/images/01-image-models.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Use it for editing too

When the download is done, click **Use**. FLUX.2 klein 4B both draws and edits from one download: open **Image edit** and click **Use** on it there as well.

![Image edit, with FLUX.2 klein 4B in use](/guides/images/02-image-edit-model.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Draw a picture

In Studio, click **Image**. Under **Type**, keep **Image**, or choose **Infographic** for a picture that lays out your sources' key points. Describe what you want under **Prompt (optional)**, and click **Generate**. Open it when it's ready.

![The picture Studio drew, open in the right panel](/guides/images/03-picture.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Describe a change

Click the round button at the bottom right, describe a change, such as "make it night time", and click **Edit**. An edit applies to the whole picture.

![The edit box over the picture, with a change typed in](/guides/images/04-edit-box.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Get the new version

The edited picture is the next version, and the earlier one stays under the version button. Edits chain: each one starts from the version you have open.

![The edited picture, shown as version 2](/guides/images/05-edited.webp)

</Step>
</Steps>
