SurfSense is a desktop app for Windows, macOS (Apple Silicon) and Linux. Add your documents, ask questions across them and get answers that cite where each point came from, then turn the same documents into slides, reports, study material and podcasts.

- **On your computer.** Your documents are read, indexed and stored on your disk. There is no account.
- **Your choice of model.** Run a model on your own computer, connect any OpenAI-compatible API, or use your ChatGPT or Claude plan.
- **Off until you say yes.** Model downloads, remote providers, update checks and usage statistics stay off until you allow them.
- **Works offline.** With a local model, SurfSense works with the network off.

<Callout title="Coming from 0.0.41?">
  SurfSense 2.0 is a rebuild, not an upgrade. The self-hosted web app, its
  connectors and messaging channels are documented under
  [SurfSense Legacy](/docs/legacy). Use the version dropdown at the top of the
  sidebar to switch between them.
</Callout>
