This page takes you from a fresh install to your first answer and your first Studio output. If you haven't installed SurfSense yet, start with [Install](/docs/v2/install). To see each step on screen, with a video, follow the [step-by-step guides](/docs/v2/guides), starting with [Set up SurfSense](/docs/v2/guides/set-up).

## Set up

The first time you open SurfSense, a short setup runs. You can't use the app until it's done, and you only see it once.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Start

On the welcome screen, click **Start setting up**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Make your privacy choices

Answer **Yes** or **No** to each of these, then click **Continue**:

- **Check for updates.** Lets SurfSense ask whether a newer version exists.
- **Share usage statistics.** Lets SurfSense send anonymous counts of how the app is used.

Nothing is sent until you choose. **Show exactly what’s sent** lists what each one sends. You can change both later in Settings. See [Privacy](/docs/v2/privacy).

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose a text generation model

This is the model that answers you in chat, and the one step you can't skip. Pick one of these:

- **On this computer.** SurfSense lists the models that fit your computer and marks one **Recommended**. Click **Download**: once it's downloaded, it's the model in use. Your chats stay on your computer. If the model you want isn't listed, click **Not listed? Search Hugging Face**.
- **Use a server.** Connect vLLM, LM Studio, OpenRouter or any other OpenAI-compatible API.
- **Use your ChatGPT plan.** Sign in with your ChatGPT account.
- **Use your Claude plan.** Use your Pro, Max or Team plan through Claude Code on this computer.

SurfSense asks before it downloads a model or sends anything to a server. [Models](/docs/v2/models) covers each option in detail. Click **Continue**.

The other kinds of model, for images, podcast voices and speech, are chosen later in Settings, when you want them.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose an embedding model

The embedding model finds what you ask about in your documents. The default works well for English. Pick a multilingual model if your documents are in other languages.

<Callout type="warn">
  You can’t change the embedding model later. Choose it with care.
</Callout>

Click **Finish**. SurfSense opens your first workspace.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Use SurfSense

<Steps>
<Step>

### Add sources

In **Sources**, open the add menu and choose **Upload files…** or **Upload folder…**, or drag files onto the list. You can also use **Add from the web…** for a web page or video, or **New note** to write one yourself.

Each source is processed on your computer before you can use it. A source shows as processing until it's ready. [Sources](/docs/v2/sources) lists the file types SurfSense reads.

</Step>
<Step>

### Ask a question

Type your question in the chat box and send it. The answer cites the sources each point came from. Click a citation to see the passage it points to.

A chat runs in one of two modes:

- **Basic (Q&A)** answers from your sources.
- **Agentic** does tasks across your files, with tools.

See [Chat](/docs/v2/chat) and [Agent](/docs/v2/agent).

</Step>
<Step>

### Make something in Studio

In **Studio**, pick a format, such as **Slides**, **PDF**, **Podcast**, **Mind map** or **Flashcards**. Choose the sources to use, add a prompt if you want to steer it, and click **Generate**.

The result appears under **Artifacts** when it's ready. Some formats need a model you skipped during setup; Studio tells you which one. See [Studio](/docs/v2/studio).

</Step>
</Steps>
