title: Workspaces
description: Keep separate sets of sources, chats and Studio outputs apart
---

A workspace keeps one research context together: its sources and folders, its chats, and what Studio made from them. A question in one workspace never searches another.

Your models, plugins, license and settings are not part of a workspace. They apply to every workspace on this computer.

Step-by-step guide, with a video: [Keep projects apart with workspaces](/docs/v2/guides/workspaces).

## Your first workspace

The first time SurfSense starts, it makes a workspace called **My Workspace**, so there is always one to open. You can rename it or add more.

## The workspace rail

Workspaces sit in the narrow rail at the far left of the window. Each one shows the first letters of its name. Click a workspace to switch to it. SurfSense remembers the last workspace you opened and returns to it the next time you start the app.

Switching does not stop anything. A reply still being written in another workspace keeps going, and its chat is marked unread when it finishes.

## Create a workspace

1. Click **+** in the workspace rail. Its tooltip reads **Create workspace**.
2. Type a name.
3. Click **Create**.

The new workspace opens straight away, empty. A name can be up to 200 characters.

## Rename a workspace

Right-click the workspace in the rail and choose **Rename**. Type the new name and click **Rename**.

## Delete a workspace

Right-click the workspace in the rail and choose **Delete**, then confirm with **Delete workspace**.

<Callout type="warn">
  Deleting a workspace permanently deletes its chats, its sources, their
  indexed data and its Studio outputs. Replies still being written in it are
  stopped first. There is no undo and no Trash.
</Callout>

Your original files elsewhere on your computer are not touched: SurfSense deletes only its own copies.
