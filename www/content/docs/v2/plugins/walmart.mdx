title: Walmart
description: Read Walmart products, search results and reviews
---

Walmart researches products from your chats. It reads product pages, search and category listings, prices, sellers, variants and availability, and pages through a product's reviews, without your Walmart account, so nothing is ever bought.

Needs a [SurfSense license](/docs/v2/license). See [SurfSense plugins](/docs/v2/plugins/surfsense-plugins) to connect it.

## Tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `scrape` | Reads products from a product, search, category or browse link, or from search terms. Each product comes with its price, seller, variants, availability and a sample of its reviews. |
| `reviews` | Pages through a product's reviews, with ratings, text, authors, verified-purchase marks, images and seller replies. Sorted most recent, most helpful, highest or lowest rated. |

## Example prompts

- "@walmart Compare the cheapest 55-inch TVs on Walmart with at least four stars."
- "Is this item in stock, and who sells it? https://www.walmart.com/ip/…"
- "Read the 100 most helpful reviews of this blender and list the common problems."
- "Search Walmart for 'air fryer' and show the top ten with prices and ratings."

## Add from the web

In **+ › Add from the web…**:

- **A product link** (`/ip/…`) becomes one source with the product and its most helpful reviews.
- **A search or category link** opens as a list of products. Add the ones you want.
- **Search Walmart itself**: pick Walmart in the **Search on** menu under the box.

## Limits

- **US site only.** It reads walmart.com.
- **A block shows as an error item.** When Walmart refuses a link, the result has an error for that link instead of a product. Try again later.
