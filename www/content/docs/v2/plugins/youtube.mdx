title: YouTube

YouTube lets you work with videos without watching every one. It reads public videos, channels and playlists with their subtitles, and the comments and replies under a video.

Needs a [SurfSense license](/docs/v2/license). See [SurfSense plugins](/docs/v2/plugins/surfsense-plugins) to connect it.

## Tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `scrape` | Reads videos from a video, channel, playlist, Shorts or hashtag link, or from a YouTube search. Each video comes with its title, description, date, length, views, likes, comment count and channel. It can also fetch subtitles in a chosen language. |
| `comments` | Reads the comments and replies under one or more videos, with authors, text, likes and dates, top comments or newest first. |

## Example prompts

- "@youtube Summarise this talk and list its main arguments: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=…"
- "List the last ten videos on @channelname with their view counts."
- "Pull the French subtitles of this video and translate the first five minutes."
- "What do the top comments under this video say about the product?"

## Add from the web

**+ › Add from the web…** doesn't use this plugin. SurfSense reads a video's transcript there from YouTube on your own connection, in the language the video speaks, with no license. See [Add from the web](/docs/v2/sources#add-from-the-web).

## Limits

- **Public videos only.**
- **A block reads as nothing found.** When YouTube refuses a request, the result can come back empty rather than as an error. Try again later.
