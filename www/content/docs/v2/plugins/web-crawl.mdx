---
title: Web Crawl
description: Read a web page, or crawl a whole site into clean text
---

Web Crawl reads the web as clean, readable text. It opens any public page, or follows a site's links to read many of its pages at once, so the agent can summarise, compare or answer questions from what it found.

Needs a [SurfSense license](/docs/v2/license). See [SurfSense plugins](/docs/v2/plugins/surfsense-plugins) to connect it.

## Tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `crawl` | Reads one or more pages as text. It can also follow links from those pages, staying on the same site, a few levels deep. Each page comes back with its title, description, author, date and text, plus its links and any contact details it lists. |

The agent can choose how many link hops to follow, how many pages to read in all, and which links to follow or skip, for example only pages under `/docs/`.

## Example prompts

- "@web-crawl Summarise https://example.com/blog/launch-post in five bullet points."
- "Read the pricing pages of these three products and compare their plans in a table: …"
- "Crawl the docs at https://docs.example.com, two levels deep, and tell me how authentication works."
- "Find the contact email and phone number on https://example.com."

## Add from the web

**+ › Add from the web…** doesn't use this plugin. SurfSense reads a page you add there on your own computer and connection, with no license. See [Add from the web](/docs/v2/sources#add-from-the-web).

## Limits

- **Public pages only.** Pages behind a login or a paywall stay out of reach.
- **No local addresses.** It refuses addresses on your own network, such as `localhost` or `192.168.…`, and names that don't resolve.
- **Crawls have a ceiling.** A crawl stops at five link hops and 200 pages. A very large crawl can time out, so ask for fewer pages if it does.
- **Only http and https** addresses can be read.
