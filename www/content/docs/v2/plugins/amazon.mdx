title: Amazon
description: Read Amazon products and search results
---

Amazon researches products from your chats. It reads product pages and search results with prices, offers from other sellers, best-seller rankings and the reviews on the page, without your Amazon account, so nothing is ever bought.

Needs a [SurfSense license](/docs/v2/license). See [SurfSense plugins](/docs/v2/plugins/surfsense-plugins) to connect it.

## Tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `scrape` | Reads products from a product, search, category or best-sellers link, or from search terms. Works on any Amazon site, such as amazon.de or amazon.co.jp. Each product comes with its title, brand, price, rating, description, details and the reviews on its page. |

The agent can also ask for offers from other sellers with their ratings, a product's variants such as sizes and colours, and prices for a delivery country and postcode.

## Example prompts

- "@amazon Compare the three best-selling robot vacuums under $300 on amazon.com."
- "What do reviewers say about this product's battery life? https://www.amazon.com/dp/…"
- "Search amazon.de for 'mechanical keyboard' and list the top ten with prices in euros."
- "Show the other sellers' offers for this ASIN and who's cheapest."

## Add from the web

In **+ › Add from the web…**:

- **A product link** becomes one source with the product and its reviews. `/dp/` and `/gp/product/` links work, as do the short links `a.co`, `amzn.to` and `amzn.eu`.
- **A search, category or best-sellers link** opens as a list of products. Add the ones you want.
- **Search Amazon itself**: pick Amazon in the **Search on** menu under the box. It searches Amazon's US store.

## Limits

- **Only the reviews on the product page.** It doesn't page through every review.
- **A block shows as an error item.** When Amazon refuses a link, the result has an error for that link instead of a product. Try again later.
