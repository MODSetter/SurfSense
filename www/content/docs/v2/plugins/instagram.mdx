title: Instagram
description: Read Instagram profiles, posts and reels
---

Instagram looks into public accounts from your chats. It reads public posts and reels from a profile or a link, finds profiles by search, and shows a profile's bio, follower and post counts and latest posts, without your own Instagram account.

Needs a [SurfSense license](/docs/v2/license). See [SurfSense plugins](/docs/v2/plugins/surfsense-plugins) to connect it.

## Tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `scrape` | Reads posts or reels from profiles, or single posts and reels by link. It can keep only posts newer than a date and leave out pinned posts. |
| `details` | Reads a profile's details: bio, follower and post counts, link and latest posts. Given keywords instead of profiles, it finds matching profiles first. |

## Example prompts

- "@instagram What has @natgeo posted this week? List each post with its caption."
- "Compare the follower counts and bios of these three accounts: …"
- "Find Instagram profiles of specialty coffee roasters in Berlin."
- "Read this reel's caption and tell me what product it promotes: https://www.instagram.com/reel/…"

## Add from the web

In **+ › Add from the web…**:

- **A post or reel link** becomes one source with its caption and details.
- **A profile link** opens as a list of its latest posts, and a profile's `/reels/` link as its latest reels.
- **Search Instagram itself**: pick Instagram in the **Search on** menu under the box. It finds profiles. Each profile then opens as a list.

Short links, such as `instagr.am/…` or share links, can't be added. Open them in your browser and copy the full link instead.

## Limits

- **What a logged-out visitor sees.** A profile shows only its latest 12 posts or 12 reels, and posts come without their comments.
- **No hashtags or places.** Instagram puts those behind a login.
- **Finding profiles goes through Google**, so it can be slower than reading a profile you name.
- **Video links expire.** A video's address is only valid for hours to days.
