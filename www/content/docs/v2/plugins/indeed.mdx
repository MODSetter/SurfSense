title: Indeed

Indeed researches jobs from your chats. It reads public job postings with their title, company, location, salary and full description, without your Indeed account, so nothing is ever applied for.

Needs a [SurfSense license](/docs/v2/license). See [SurfSense plugins](/docs/v2/plugins/surfsense-plugins) to connect it.

## Tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `scrape` | Reads jobs from a search link, a company's jobs page or a single job link, or runs a job search. It works on Indeed's site for any country. Each job comes with its title, company, location and salary, and, if asked, its full description, pay and company rating. |

A search can be narrowed by location and radius, job type such as full-time or contract, experience level, remote or hybrid work, and how many days ago the job was posted.

## Example prompts

- "@indeed Find remote senior data engineer jobs in Germany posted in the last week."
- "What's the typical salary for a UX designer in Toronto, based on current listings?"
- "List the jobs open at this company and group them by team: https://www.indeed.com/cmp/…/jobs"
- "Read this job posting and tell me which of my skills match: https://www.indeed.com/viewjob?jk=…"

## Add from the web

In **+ › Add from the web…**:

- **A job link**, any Indeed link with `jk=` in it, becomes one source with the full posting.
- **A search or company page link** opens as a list of jobs. Add the ones you want.
- **Search Indeed itself**: pick Indeed in the **Search on** menu under the box. It searches Indeed's US site. For another country, paste a search link from that country's Indeed site.

## Limits

- **Public postings only.**
- **Indeed can refuse.** When Indeed keeps blocking, the call fails with that reason. Try again later.
