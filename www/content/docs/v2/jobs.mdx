A job does paperwork from your firm's own documents:

- **Security questionnaire** fills in a questionnaire a customer sent you, as an Excel workbook, from your policies, with the source of each answer. What it can't back with a quote is left for a person to check.
- **Contract redline** reviews a contract you received, as a Word file, against your firm's playbook, and marks up a copy with tracked changes and comments. See [Review a contract](#review-a-contract).

Each run makes a revised copy of your file in [Studio](/docs/v2/studio). Your own file is never changed.

Step-by-step guides, with videos: [Start a trial and add your license](/docs/v2/guides/business-trial), [Fill in a security questionnaire from your policies](/docs/v2/guides/security-questionnaire) and [Redline a contract against your playbook](/docs/v2/guides/contract-redline).

## What you need

Jobs come with a SurfSense Business license, and with its free 14-day trial. See [pricing](/pricing), and [License](/docs/v2/license) to add yours.

A job answers with the chat model selected in Settings. A model that runs on your computer keeps your documents on it, but it is usually smaller and leaves more questions for a person to answer.

## Open Jobs

Click **Jobs** in the left sidebar, below **Plugins**. Each job has a row. When your license is what stops a job, Jobs says so once, in a box above the jobs, and marks each job it stops: **Business** for a job your license doesn't include, **Locked** for the rest. A job that is ready shows **Run**; one your license includes but that isn't downloaded yet offers **Download** on its own row:

| When | The row says | Buttons |
|---|---|---|
| The job is on this computer and your license includes it | The job's version | **Run**, **Settings** |
| Your license includes the job, but it isn't downloaded yet | "Your license includes this job. Download it to run it on this computer." | **Download**, **Import pack file**, **Contact support** |
| You have no license | "Jobs come with a SurfSense Business license. Try it free for 14 days." | **Start 14-day trial**, **See pricing**, **Add license** |
| A trial license that doesn't include the job | "Your license doesn’t include the job marked Business. Jobs come with a Business license, which you can try free for 14 days." | **Start 14-day trial**, **See pricing**, **Replace license** |
| Another plan, such as Individual or Team | "Your Individual license doesn’t include the job marked Business: jobs come with Business. If your license has moved to Business, Check for renewal fetches its new file." | **Check for renewal**, **See pricing** |
| Your 14-day trial has ended | "Your 14-day trial has ended. To keep running jobs, buy a Business license, then replace the trial’s license with the one we email you." | **See pricing**, **Replace license** |
| Your license has ended | "Your license has ended, so the job marked Locked can’t run. If it was renewed, Check for renewal fetches the new file." | **Check for renewal**, **See pricing** |
| Your computer's clock is behind | "This computer’s clock is behind the last time SurfSense ran. Set the clock right to run the job marked Locked." | None. Set the clock right, and the jobs run again. |

**Start 14-day trial** opens the [downloads page](/downloads), where you ask for the trial by email. **See pricing** opens [pricing](/pricing), and **Contact support** the [contact page](/contact). **Add license** and **Replace license** open Settings › License.

## Get the job

When you add or replace a license that includes jobs, SurfSense downloads them from its license server, `api.surfsense.com`. The first time, SurfSense asks "Allow downloading your jobs?": choose **Allow**. If you chose **Cancel**, choose **Download** on the job's row when you're ready.

After that, SurfSense keeps the job up to date. It checks for a newer version each time it starts and once a day, and after you choose **Check for renewal**. To check now, open Settings › License and choose **Check for updates** under **Jobs on this computer**, which lists each job your license includes with its version, or "Not downloaded yet".

SurfSense never replaces a job while a run of it is under way. If a check comes during a run, SurfSense checks again on its own once the run ends.

### Without internet

No internet on this computer? Choose **Contact support** and ask SurfSense support for the job pack file. Then choose **Import pack file**, on the job's row in Jobs or in Settings › License, and pick the `.zip` file. Importing sends nothing.

SurfSense installs a job pack only if SurfSense signed it, and never one older than the version you have.

## Run a job

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Add the questionnaire you received, as an Excel workbook (`.xlsx`), and your firm's policies to **Sources**, and wait until they're ready. Save a Word or older Excel file as `.xlsx` first.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    In **Jobs**, choose **Run** beside **Security questionnaire**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Under **Workbook to fill in**, pick the workbook: each run fills in one. The newest one ticked in **Sources**, or else the newest, is picked to start with, and when your sources hold a single workbook, it's the one the run fills in. **Add a workbook** adds another, or adds one when none of your sources is a workbook.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Under **Your firm’s documents to answer from**, tick the documents the answers should rest on. The sources ticked in **Sources** start ticked, except other workbooks. **Add documents** adds more.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Choose **Start**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The form names the model that answers, which is the chat model selected in Settings, and the model that checks each answer again, when the job's [settings](#job-settings) name one. A model that doesn't run on your computer receives the questions and passages from your documents. With no chat model chosen, **Choose a model** opens Settings.

### While it runs

Jobs shows how far the run has got, such as "40 of 120 questions". **Cancel run** stops it, and nothing is written. You can close Jobs and keep working, but keep SurfSense open until the run is done: quitting stops it.

When the run is done, Jobs says how many questions SurfSense answered, how many need a person, and how many suggested answers wait for you to confirm. **Open in Studio** opens the copy.

If the run stops, Jobs says why, with **Run again** and **Choose another workbook**. A workbook in which SurfSense finds no questions stops the run at once: it needs a column of questions and a column for answers, or answer dropdowns.

## What a job writes

In the copy, SurfSense writes:

- each answer in its own cell, spelled as the cell's dropdown spells it;
- a comment and the answer's source, in the workbook's own comment and evidence columns where they're empty;
- two sheets of its own after yours: an answers sheet, with each answer's source and the quote it rests on, and an exceptions sheet, with every question a person should look at, and why. Jobs names both when the run is done.

SurfSense writes Yes only where a passage in your documents says so, word for word, and a second check agrees. A question it can't back is left for a person. Hidden rows and columns, and rows that are locked or already answered, are left as they were.

Download the copy in Studio with **With changes**. Before you send it, check SurfSense's two sheets, and delete them if the recipient shouldn't see them.

## Confirm suggested answers

Some answers are suggestions: SurfSense found passages that may back them, but leaves the choice to you. They're on the exceptions sheet with the rest of what a person should look at.

Once you've checked them, choose "Confirm 3 suggested answers", with your own count, in Jobs when the run is done, or over the copy's newest version in Studio. SurfSense lists them and asks first. Choose **Confirm**: each one is written into its cell in a new version of the copy, and the exceptions sheet notes that a person confirmed it. Earlier versions stay under **Versions** in Studio.

## Review a contract

**Contract redline** checks a contract you received against your firm's playbook, one review point at a time, and marks up a copy where the contract departs from it. Its review points are those of a non-disclosure agreement, such as what counts as confidential, notice of a compelled disclosure, return or destruction, and survival.

Your playbook is your firm's written positions, its own standard agreement, or both, added to **Sources** like any other document. SurfSense never makes up a position: where your playbook says nothing on a point, the contract is left as it is there.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Add the contract, as a Word file (`.docx`), and your playbook to **Sources**, and wait until they're ready. Save an older Word file (`.doc`) as `.docx` first.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    In **Jobs**, choose **Run** beside **Contract redline**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Under **Contract to review**, pick the contract: each run reviews one. **Add a contract** adds another.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Under **Your firm’s playbook**, tick your playbook's documents. The sources ticked in **Sources** start ticked, another Word file too, since it may be your own standard agreement. **Add playbook documents** adds more.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Choose **Start**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Jobs counts review points as the run goes, such as "3 of 17 review points". When it's done, it says how many points were changed, how many meet your playbook, how many are not in it, and which need a person.

In the copy, each change is a tracked change by SurfSense, which you accept or reject in Studio or in Word:

- where a clause departs from your playbook, SurfSense rewrites it, and only the words that change are marked;
- a term your playbook asks for and the contract lacks is added as a new paragraph;
- each change carries a comment the other side can read, giving its reason;
- SurfSense's reasoning, with the words of your playbook it rests on, is an internal comment. Internal comments stay in Studio: every download leaves them out.

A point SurfSense can't settle, or whose change it can't place in the contract, is left for a person, with an internal comment saying why. SurfSense reviews and changes the body of the contract only, never its headers, footers or footnotes, and reviews a contract that already holds tracked changes as it reads with them.

Download the copy in Studio with **With changes**. In the job's **Settings**, "Explain each change to the other side" is on to start with; turn it off and every comment SurfSense writes stays internal.

## Recent runs

**Recent runs** in Jobs lists your latest runs, newest first, each with when it started, how far it got, and how many suggestions wait to be confirmed. Choose one to open it. Older runs are in Studio.

## Job settings

**Settings** beside a job holds your firm's choices for every run of it:

- the switches the job offers, each with what it does;
- **Second check**, for a job that checks its answers, such as Security questionnaire: SurfSense checks each answer a second time before writing it, with **The answering model** unless you choose another, on your computer or from a provider you connected. One that doesn't run on your computer receives each question and its passages.

Choose **Save**. A change applies to the runs you start after it.

If you **Disconnect** the provider of the **Second check** model, in Settings › Text gen, SurfSense won't start the job until you choose another model for **Second check** and choose **Save**. Then start a new run: **Run again** on an earlier run uses the model it started with.

## In a chat

In an [Agentic](/docs/v2/chat#two-modes) chat, tick the workbook and your policies in **Sources**, and ask the agent to fill in the questionnaire. Its step reads "Filled in" with the workbook's name, and opens the copy in Studio. Once you've checked the suggested answers, ask the agent to confirm them.

For a contract, tick it and your playbook, and ask the agent to review the contract against your playbook. Its step reads "Reviewed" with the contract's name.

A **Basic (Q&A)** chat can't run jobs. Use **Jobs** in the sidebar instead.

## When your license ends

When your license ends, its jobs stop running, and Jobs says why above them. Everything a job made stays in Studio. The job stays on your computer too, so it runs again once a license includes it.

- A Business license ends 30 days after the year you paid for, and Settings › License shows **Expires** with the date. If it was renewed, **Check for renewal** fetches the new file.
- When your 14-day trial ends, buy a Business license with **See pricing**, then add the license we email you with **Replace license**.

|---|---|

## What jobs send

Getting a job sends SurfSense's license server, `api.surfsense.com`, your license key, the job's code, the version you already have and the kinds of job your version of SurfSense can run, with your IP address. Nothing is sent until you allow it, and you can untick **License server** in Settings › Network at any time. Importing a pack file sends nothing.

Running a job sends SurfSense nothing. Your documents are read on your computer, and go only to the models you chose for the job, the one that answers and the one that checks, when they don't run on your computer. See [privacy](/docs/v2/privacy).
