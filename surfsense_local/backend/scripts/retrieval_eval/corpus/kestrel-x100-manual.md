# Kestrel X100 Blender — Owner's Manual

## In the box

The Kestrel X100 ships with the motor base, a 1.2 litre Tritan jar, a plain lid without a measuring cap, and this manual. The X100 has no tamper; do not substitute a spoon or spatula while the motor is running.

## Assembly

Stand the motor base on a dry, level surface. Line up the arrow on the jar with the unlock mark on the base, lower the jar straight down, and turn it clockwise until it stops. The appliance will not start unless the jar is seated.

## Using the blender

Add liquids first, then soft ingredients. The X100 is not rated for ice or frozen fruit; use the X200 or above for those. Do not fill past the maximum line.

The X100 has three buttons rather than a dial: low, high, and pulse. There is no variable speed control on this model.

## Cleaning

Rinse the jar with warm water immediately after each use. For a full clean, half fill the jar with warm water, add one drop of washing-up liquid, and run on low for thirty seconds. Unlike the larger models, the X100 blade assembly is dishwasher safe on the top rack.

## Warranty

The Kestrel X100 is covered for 18 months from the date of purchase against defects in materials and workmanship. This is shorter than the cover on the X200 and above. Keep your receipt as proof of purchase. The warranty does not cover damage from commercial use or from blending ice, which this model is not rated for.

## Service parts

Replacement jars and lids are available for three years from the date this model was discontinued. The X100 jar is not interchangeable with the X200 jar, because the drive coupling differs.
