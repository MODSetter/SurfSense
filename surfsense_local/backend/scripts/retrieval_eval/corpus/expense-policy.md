# Expense Policy

## Who this covers

All employees and contractors travelling or buying on the company's behalf. Anything bought for personal use is never claimable, whatever the amount.

## Approval thresholds

Spending up to 200 euros needs no prior approval; claim it afterwards with a receipt. Between 200 and 2,000 euros needs your manager's approval before you spend. Above 2,000 euros needs a director's approval before you spend, and finance will reject a claim approved after the fact.

## Receipts

Every claim needs an itemised receipt. A card statement is not a receipt, because it does not show what was bought. Where a receipt is genuinely unavailable, write a short note explaining why; finance accepts up to three such notes a year per person.

## Travel

Book flights in economy on any route under six hours. Premium economy is allowed above six hours, and business class only with a director's approval before booking. Trains are preferred to flights where the journey is under four hours door to door.

Hotels are capped at 180 euros a night in Lisbon, Berlin and Warsaw, and 260 euros a night in London and Zurich. Where no room is available under the cap, book the cheapest available and note it on the claim.

## Meals

Meals while travelling are reimbursed at actual cost up to 60 euros a day, receipts required. There is no per-diem. Alcohol is not reimbursed except at a client dinner, which needs the client named on the claim.

## Submitting a claim

Claims are submitted monthly and must reach finance within 60 days of the spend. A claim older than that needs a director's approval to be paid at all.
