# Returns Policy (current, from 1 March 2026)

*This policy replaces the 2024 returns policy for all orders placed on or after 1 March 2026.*

## Window

From 1 March 2026 the return window is 45 days from delivery. This replaces the 14-day window in the 2024 policy. The 45 days run from the date the carrier records the parcel as delivered.

## Condition

Items should be returned in a condition that allows resale. Unlike the 2024 policy, the original packaging is no longer required, provided the item itself is undamaged and all accessories are present. Use any sturdy outer box.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within five working days of the item arriving back at the warehouse. Where the original payment method has since been closed, we issue account credit instead.

## Return postage

Return postage is free for every order over 40 euros. Below that, the customer pays return postage unless the item is faulty.

## Exclusions

Perishable goods, personalised items and gift cards cannot be returned. This does not affect your statutory rights where an item is faulty.

## Faulty items

An item that develops a fault outside the returns window is handled under its warranty rather than this policy. Warranty terms vary by product and are printed in each product's manual.
