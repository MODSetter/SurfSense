# Kestrel X200 Blender — Owner's Manual

## In the box

The Kestrel X200 ships with the motor base, a 1.8 litre Tritan jar, a vented lid with a removable measuring cap, a tamper, and this manual. If any part is missing, contact the retailer you bought it from before using the appliance.

## Assembly

Stand the motor base on a dry, level surface away from the edge of the counter. Line up the arrow on the jar with the unlock mark on the base, lower the jar straight down, and turn it clockwise until it stops. The appliance will not start unless the jar is seated, which is deliberate.

## Using the blender

Add liquids first, then soft ingredients, then frozen items and ice last. Do not fill past the maximum line, which sits about three centimetres below the rim. Hold the lid down for the first few seconds of any thick mixture. Use the tamper only through the measuring cap opening, never with the cap removed.

The speed dial runs from 1 to 10. Start every mixture at 1 and raise the speed once the ingredients are moving. Pulse is the momentary position to the left of 1; it will not latch.

## Cleaning

Rinse the jar with warm water immediately after each use, before anything dries onto the blade. For a full clean, half fill the jar with warm water, add one drop of washing-up liquid, and run at speed 4 for thirty seconds. The blade assembly is not dishwasher safe and should be dried by hand. Wipe the motor base with a damp cloth; never immerse it.

## Storage

Store the jar unlocked from the base so the seal is not compressed. If the appliance will be unused for more than a month, remove the blade assembly and dry it thoroughly.

## Warranty

The Kestrel X200 is covered for 27 months from the date of purchase against defects in materials and workmanship. Keep your receipt as proof of purchase. The warranty does not cover damage from commercial use, from running the appliance dry, or from any repair carried out by someone other than an approved service centre.

To make a claim, contact the retailer first. Where the retailer has closed, write to the service address in the back of this manual quoting the serial number from the underside of the motor base.

## Service parts

Replacement jars, lids, tampers and blade assemblies are available for seven years from the date this model was discontinued. Order them by the part number printed inside the lid.
