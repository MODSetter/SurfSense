# Travel Booking Policy (2025)

*Superseded on 1 January 2026. Kept for reference while 2025 claims are still open.*

## Booking window

Book flights at least 21 days ahead. Bookings inside 21 days need a manager's approval, recorded on the booking itself rather than by email.

## Travel agent

All flights and hotels go through the agent, Meridian Travel. Direct bookings are not reimbursed except where the agent cannot offer a route, which they confirm in writing.

## Trains

Trains may be booked directly without the agent, in standard class. First class is not reimbursed.

## Airport transfers

Taxis to and from airports are reimbursed. Rideshare is not, because the receipts do not itemise.

## Cancellations

Cancel through the agent. Where a trip is cancelled for a business reason, the cost is written off to the team's budget; where it is cancelled for a personal reason, the traveller repays it.
