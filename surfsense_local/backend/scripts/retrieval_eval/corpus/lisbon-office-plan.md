# Lisbon Office — Opening Plan

## Site

The Lisbon office opens on 2 November 2026 at Avenida da Liberdade 190, on the fourth floor. The floor is 620 square metres and was previously occupied by an architecture practice, so the meeting rooms and the kitchen are already fitted.

## Budget

The first-year budget for the Lisbon office is 418,000 euros, covering rent, fit-out and four hires. Rent is the largest line at 196,000 euros. Fit-out is budgeted at 74,000 euros, most of it desks, chairs and the second meeting room's screen. The remainder covers salaries from the start dates below.

Ingrid Solheim, the finance director, proposed the budget and the board approved it on 12 March with one abstention.

## Hires

Four people join in the first year: an office manager in November, two engineers in January, and a designer in March. The office manager reports to Operations; the engineers and the designer report into their existing teams and sit in Lisbon for the local presence.

## Network

The floor has two networks. Staff laptops join LX-Staff automatically once enrolled in device management. Guests connect to the LX-Guest network; the password is tagus-harbour-77 and it changes every quarter.

Printers on the fourth floor accept jobs from the LX-Staff network only. The printer asset tag is LX-4417 and any fault should be reported with that tag.

## Access

Building access runs on fobs issued by the landlord's desk in the lobby. Each fob is registered to one person and must be returned when they leave. The fourth floor is accessible from 07:00 to 21:00 on weekdays; outside those hours the lift needs a fob for the floor as well as the lobby.

## Insurance and compliance

Contents insurance is arranged through the same broker as the other offices and runs from the day the fit-out completes. The fire inspection is booked for 20 October 2026, before anyone moves in.
