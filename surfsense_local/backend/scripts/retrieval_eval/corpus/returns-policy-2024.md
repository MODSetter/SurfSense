# Returns Policy (2024)

*This policy applied to orders placed before 1 March 2026. It is kept for reference. See the current returns policy for orders placed since.*

## Window

Customers may return any item within 14 days of delivery. The 14 days run from the date the carrier records the parcel as delivered, not from the date of the order.

## Condition

Items must be in their original packaging, with any seals unbroken and all accessories present. We cannot accept a return where the packaging has been discarded, because the item cannot then be resold.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within ten working days of the item arriving back at the warehouse. The original delivery charge is refunded only where the item was faulty or was not the item ordered.

## Return postage

The customer pays return postage unless the item is faulty. Where an item is faulty, we send a prepaid label by email within one working day of the claim being accepted.

## Exclusions

Perishable goods, personalised items and gift cards cannot be returned. This does not affect your statutory rights where an item is faulty.

## Faulty items

An item that develops a fault outside the returns window is handled under its warranty rather than this policy. Warranty terms vary by product and are printed in each product's manual.
