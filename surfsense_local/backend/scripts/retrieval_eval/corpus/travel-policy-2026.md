# Travel Booking Policy (current, from 1 January 2026)

*This policy replaces the 2025 travel booking policy for all travel booked on or after 1 January 2026.*

## Booking window

Book flights at least 10 days ahead. This replaces the 21-day window in the 2025 policy. Bookings inside 10 days need a manager's approval, recorded on the booking itself.

## Travel agent

The agent relationship with Meridian Travel ended on 31 December 2025. Book flights and hotels yourself through the self-serve tool, and claim them as ordinary expenses. There is no longer an agent to route bookings through.

## Trains

Trains may be booked directly, in standard class. First class is reimbursed only where standard sold out and the journey is over three hours, with a note on the claim.

## Airport transfers

Taxis and rideshare are both reimbursed, since the self-serve tool now collects itemised rideshare receipts automatically. This reverses the 2025 exclusion.

## Cancellations

Cancel in the self-serve tool. Where a trip is cancelled for a business reason, the cost is written off to the team's budget; where it is cancelled for a personal reason, the traveller repays it.
