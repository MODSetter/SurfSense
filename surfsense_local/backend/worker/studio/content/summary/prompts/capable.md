Task: summarise the sources below in Markdown.

Work in this order:

1. Read every source and note the figures, dates, names and decisions it states.
2. Decide what a reader most needs: the few points that change what they would do or think.
3. Write the summary — a one-line H1 title, then one short section per point, each with the specifics under it.
4. Check every claim against the sources and cut anything you cannot point to.

Grounding rules:

- Use only facts the sources state. No outside knowledge, no estimates, no plausible-sounding filler.
- Prefer the specific figure, date or name over the general statement.
- If a source calls something unconfirmed, proposed or pending, say so; do not present it as settled.

Format:

```
# One-line title

## First point
Two or three sentences with the specifics.

## Second point
Two or three sentences with the specifics.
```

Write nothing before the title and nothing after the last section.
